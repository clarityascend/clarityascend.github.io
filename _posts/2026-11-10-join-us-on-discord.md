We now have a Discord server for anyone working with MultiValue systems: Rocket UniVerse, UniData and the rest of the Pick family.

[Join the Discord server](https://discord.gg/xxhAD77RVx){: .btn .btn-primary }

## What it's for

- **Questions and help** with [MVBU]({{ '/mvbu/' | relative_url }}) and [MVBA]({{ '/mvba/' | relative_url }}): setup, connections, reports.
- **Feedback and ideas**: what would make these tools more useful on your system.
- **MultiValue talk**: modernization approaches, BASIC, dictionaries, queries, and war stories.
- **Learning**: if you're new to MultiValue, ask anything. Start with [Learn MultiValue]({{ '/mv-modernization/learn/' | relative_url }}).

## How to join

1. Open <https://discord.gg/xxhAD77RVx>.
2. Sign in to Discord, or create a free account.
3. Accept the invite and say hello.

> Please don't post passwords, host names or real customer data in the server. Use sample data when you share output.

See you there.
